Academic Profile & Focus

Bridging Fundamental Physics & Functional Devices

Doctoral researcher affiliated with the School of Physics, University of Hyderabad, conducting advanced experimental investigations in the CASEST laboratory under the guidance of Prof. K. C. James Raju.

My research centers on high-frequency microwave characterization of functional materials, ferromagnetic thin film engineering, and magnetoelectric device physics. By uniting specialized vacuum deposition (RF/DC Sputtering, Pulsed Laser Deposition) with advanced high-frequency vector network analyzer diagnostics (VNA-FMR, cavity perturbation, and broadband coaxial/waveguide techniques), I investigate fundamental spin dynamics, damping constants, and microwave losses.

"Engineering soft magnetic FeGaB thin films with tailored in-plane anisotropy, low Gilbert damping, and giant magnetostriction for compact multiferroic sensors and tunable microwave devices."

Beyond experimental materials synthesis, I have conceived, fabricated, and standardized multiple in-house experimental setups—including a custom Vector Network Analyzer Ferromagnetic Resonance (VNA-FMR) system, a miniature probe-station electromagnet, a dynamic magnetoelectric coefficient testing bench, and precision low-cost optoelectronic devices.

Core Research Thrusts

FeGaB Thin Film Magnetism

Optimization of RF-sputtered Galfenol-boron (FeGaB) films. Tailoring static magnetic coercivity, saturation magnetization, and in-plane uniaxial anisotropy for high-frequency sensor integration.

  • Swift Heavy Ion (SHI) irradiation tuning at IUAC Delhi
  • Pt/FeGaB/Pt tri-layer interface optimization
  • Low-damping Galfenol alloys lightly doped with Cu/Ag

Broadband VNA-FMR & Spin Dynamics

Frequency and field-domain Ferromagnetic Resonance using coplanar waveguides (CPW) coupled to a Vector Network Analyzer, extracting intrinsic damping and spectroscopic Landé g-factors.

  • Precise determination of Landé g-factor and Kittel resonance
  • Gilbert damping parameter (α) extraction up to 20 GHz
  • Angular-dependent in-plane resonance spectroscopy

Microwave Absorbers & EMI Shielding

Synthesizing lightweight polymer nanocomposites and substituted spinels (ferrites, rGO, MWCNTs) for reflection loss and electromagnetic interference shielding across X (8–12 GHz) and Ku (12–18 GHz) bands.

  • BARC & CSIR-AMPRI collaborative high-impact papers
  • Quarter-wavelength thickness matching models
  • Defect-induced dipolar polarization mechanisms

Magnetoelectric Coupling Devices

Investigation of composite multiferroic heterostructures pairing magnetostrictive FeGaB alloys with piezoelectric and ferroelectric matrices for magnetic field sensors and energy harvesting.

  • Transverse and longitudinal ME coefficient (αME) evaluation
  • Dynamic lock-in amplifier voltage detection
  • Micro-cantilever MEMS sensor design (CeNSE IISc)

Oxide Dielectrics & Phase Transitions

Pulsed Laser Deposition and thermal annealing of functional oxides including BaSrTiO3 (BST), Na0.5Bi0.5TiO3 (NBT), and strain-induced insulator-metal transitions in VO2 thin films.

  • Microwave dielectric tunability vs oxygen partial pressure
  • Laser crystallization and rapid microwave annealing
  • Critical review on VO2 insulator-metal phase transition

Scientific Instrumentation & Open Code

Designing affordable open hardware, embedded microcontrollers, and Python calculation suites to empower laboratories with automated testing benches and reproducible data pipelines.

  • Arduino-controlled optical chopper up to 2.8 kHz
  • Miniature 800 Oe probe-station electromagnets
  • Python suites for MOKE, FMR, and EMI shielding data

High Frequency Magnetics

VNA-FMR Broadband Ferromagnetic Resonance

1/2 • In-House CPW Bench 🔍 Enlarge
2/2 • Calibration & Standardization 🔍 Enlarge

Developed a comprehensive frequency-domain and field-domain Ferromagnetic Resonance (FMR) measurement system. Utilizes a high-frequency coplanar waveguide (CPW) connected to a Vector Network Analyzer (VNA) positioned within a calibrated electromagnet to measure S-parameters (S21, S12) across microwave frequency ranges.

Frequency Band 0.1 GHz – 20 GHz
Excitation CPW Transmission Line
Key Extraction Landé g-factor, ΔH, α
Application FeGaB, Thin Film Alloys

Standardized for research papers published in Journal of Physics D and Journal of Applied Physics.

Multiferroics

Magnetoelectric Dynamic Voltage Facility

Dynamic ME Bench 🔍 Enlarge

Designed an automated magnetoelectric voltage measurement bench to evaluate the dynamic ME voltage coefficient (αME = δV / (t · δHac)) in layered magnetostrictive-piezoelectric composites. Features superimposed DC bias magnetic fields and high-precision Helmholtz AC excitation with lock-in detection.

DC Bias Field 0 to 4000 Oe
AC Modulation Helmholtz Coils (1–10 Oe)
Signal Recovery Lock-in Amplifier
Output Parameter αME (mV/cm·Oe)

Automated in LabVIEW for rapid field-sweep curves and resonance peak tracking.

Open Science Tools

Open-Source Scientific Software & Repositories

Freely accessible Python and microcontroller repositories authored by Arun B to automate laboratory data analysis and calculations.

Python

MOKE

Calculates and plots Kerr rotation and Kerr ellipticity from raw Magneto-Optical Kerr Effect data collected using a Hinds Photoelastic Modulator (PEM) and dual-phase lock-in amplifier.

View on GitHub →
Python

EMI-Shielding-Effectiveness-Calculator

Directly extracts reflection shielding (SER), absorption shielding (SEA), multiple internal reflection shielding (SEM), and total shielding (SET) from VNA scattering S-parameters (S11, S21).

View on GitHub →
Python

EM-Material-Studio

A comprehensive Python package for electromagnetic material characterization, reflection loss mapping, and impedance matching calculations across broadband frequencies.

View on GitHub →
Python

lambda_g_calculator

Calculates guided wavelength (λg), cutoff frequency (fc), and propagation constant in standard rectangular waveguides (WR-90, WR-62, WR-42) for microwave testing.

View on GitHub →
G-Code / Embedded

RotationFMR

Automated stepper-motor stage control for in-plane angular dependent Ferromagnetic Resonance measurements, executing precise angle increments with synchronized VNA acquisition triggers.
